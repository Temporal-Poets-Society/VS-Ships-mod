Caravel charter crafting uses five material-bundle recipes (planking, frames, spars, sails, rigging) at about
the ship's true material cost (docs/proposals/caravel-charter-bom.md). She launches without guns; bronze guns are
crafted and mounted on her gun places aboard (SH-0012). A battery bundle kept from 0.1.0 unpacks into its five guns.
A later proper staged shipbuilding process replaces the temporary charter route.
Launch: about 2 seconds holding right-click (native constant 1.5 s), facing open water at least 3 blocks deep
across the full hull. Current draft is 2.75 blocks. Native collision/keel checks still decide valid launch positions;
3 blocks is practical guidance, not a promise that every irregular/shallow shoreline passes.
/ships board needs the gamemode privilege (creative or admins) from 0.1.0.
