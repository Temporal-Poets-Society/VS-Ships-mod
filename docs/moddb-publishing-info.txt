across the full hull. Current draft is 2.75 blocks. Native collision/keel checks still decide valid launch positions;
3 blocks is practical guidance, not a promise that every irregular/shallow shoreline passes.
/ships board needs the gamemode privilege (creative or admins) from 0.1.0.
